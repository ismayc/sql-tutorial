## Grouping

### Grouping 1: Grouping towns by state

<SqlExercise
  id="example38"
  db="pnw_database.sqlite"
  solution={`SELECT state, COUNT(*) AS total_towns
  FROM pnw_towns
 GROUP BY state;`}
/>

### Grouping 2: Grouping and ordering counties by state and average population

<SqlExercise
  id="example39"
  db="pnw_database.sqlite"
  solution={`SELECT state, AVG(population_2022) AS avg_population
  FROM pnw_counties
 GROUP BY state
 ORDER BY avg_population DESC;`}
  requireOrdering
/>
