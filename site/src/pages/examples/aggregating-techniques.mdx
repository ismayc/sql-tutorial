## Numerical summaries

### Aggregating 1: Average population across all towns in Washington

<SqlExercise
  id="example27"
  db="pnw_database.sqlite"
  solution={`SELECT AVG(population_2020_census) AS avg_population
  FROM pnw_towns
 WHERE state = 'Washington';`}
/>

<SqlExercise

<SqlExercise

    FROM pnw_towns;`}
